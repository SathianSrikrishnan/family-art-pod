How sharing works

Ask the recipient for their Solid WebID—their account's identity link. Enter it beside a drawing, choose Share, then copy and send the invitation link. They sign in with that same account to view it.

Make private stops future access. It cannot remove copies someone has already saved.

About this trial

WAC-compatible Pods only. Start with sample artwork. No payments or public gallery. Your Pod provider can access stored files; this is not end-to-end encryption. Reconnect after a page reload; your photos remain in your Pod.
