Can’t open it?

Check your signed-in account under Your Pod is connected. Send the parent your WebID using Copy my WebID. The parent must share with that exact identity. If they made the drawing private again, the invitation no longer works.

How sharing works

1. Ask the person for their WebID. Their Pod account has a Copy WebID button. They need their own Solid account.

2. Paste their WebID beside one drawing and choose Share drawing. This replaces the previous recipient for that drawing.

3. Copy the invitation and send it yourself. They sign in with the invited account and choose Open drawing.

Make private stops future access. It cannot remove copies someone has already saved.

About this trial

WAC-compatible Pods only. Start with sample artwork. No payments or public gallery. Your Pod provider can access stored files; this is not end-to-end encryption. After reconnecting, we look for your artwork folders in your Pod. If several are found, you choose one. Only folders created by this app and with private owner permissions qualify. The trial has been tested with Private Data Pod and a local Community Solid Server; other providers are not yet verified.
