Connecting…

1. Connect your Pod

Your Pod provider handles sign-in. This app never asks for your password. Reconnect after a page reload; your saved drawings remain in your Pod.

2. Make a private collection

Sharing selects one community per drawing. Making it private removes community grants. Only this new collection's permissions are managed here. Other folders in your Pod are untouched.

3. Add a little masterpiece

Photos are resized and re-encoded in your browser before upload, removing embedded metadata. Titles and originals stay on your device.
